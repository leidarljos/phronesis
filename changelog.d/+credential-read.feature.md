The pack refuses a read or an upload of a credential store: a file reader (cat, head, grep, cp, dd, base64 and the rest), a `<` redirect, or a curl or wget upload naming `.ssh`, `.aws`, `.gnupg`, `/etc/shadow` and the other stores. A `wc` that only counts is `credential-count`, which a host may publish as a warning. This is the rule ljos-policyd's built-in table carried, case for case.
