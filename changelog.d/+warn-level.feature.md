A rule can warn instead of refuse: a hit tagged `:warn` runs the line and the entry answers `allow` with `warn: ID`, unless another rule refuses it. A download piped into Python warns as `curl-pipe-shell`; a `find` delete inside the work tree or under /tmp warns and one outside it refuses; an interpreter reading its program from `/proc/self/fd/0` or `/dev/fd/0` reads the pipe. A refusal anywhere on a line wins over a warning, and the irreversible checks run first.
