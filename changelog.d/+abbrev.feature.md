`rm` and `git` long options are read with their unique abbreviations (`rm --rec --for`, `git push --mir`, `git reset --har`). `git checkout -f` and `git switch --discard-changes` warn as `git-discard-worktree`, and `git-filter-repo` warns as `git-history-rewrite`. A delete behind a wrapper or in a `sh -c` script under `find -exec` or `xargs` is `find-exec-rm` or `feeder-rm`. A find `-delete` before its name test deletes everything it reaches.
